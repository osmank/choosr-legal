How do I remove ads?

Purchase NOSLOW+ from the STORE screen. It permanently removes interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads (revive, extra Daily Challenge attempt, double Ember) stay available either way, since they're always your choice, never required.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open STORE and tap "Restore Purchases" near the bottom. This restores NOSLOW+ if you've reinstalled the app or switched devices. Ember packs are consumable and are not restored, as they're already added to your balance at the time of purchase.

How does the Daily Challenge work?

Everyone plays the exact same run each day, generated from that day's seed. You get one free attempt; extra attempts cost Ember or a rewarded ad, up to 5 per day. Scores reset at midnight UTC.

How do I delete my data?

NOSLOW has no account and no developer-run server — your progress lives on your device only. Deleting the app deletes this data. To remove your Game Center activity, contact Apple directly; to request anything else, email us above.
