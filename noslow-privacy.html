Privacy Policy — NOSLOW

Last updated: July 20, 2026

1. Introduction

NOSLOW ("we", "our", or "us") is developed by an independent developer. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use our mobile application, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

2. Information we collect

NOSLOW has no account system, and we do not directly collect personal information. The following third-party services used in the app may process a small amount of data on their own:

  • Google AdMob — Serves optional, in-run ads (revive, extra Daily Challenge attempt, double Ember). We request non-personalized ads only, so AdMob uses only a device advertising identifier for ad delivery and fraud prevention, not for building a personalized profile.
  • Apple Game Center — If enabled on your device, collects your Game Center player identifier and display name to power leaderboards (endless, daily, and per-arena boards). Governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Apple In-App Purchases — Purchase history is processed entirely by Apple. We receive only a transaction result to confirm your purchase; we never see or store payment details.

3. Advertising

NOSLOW shows ads served by Google AdMob at a few clearly optional moments in the game. Because we request non-personalized ads only, iOS does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt — no cross-app tracking profile is built for you. You can remove interstitial ads at any time by purchasing NOSLOW+; the optional rewarded ads (revive, extra Daily Challenge try, double Ember) stay available either way, since they are always your choice.

4. In-App Purchases

NOSLOW offers optional in-app purchases: Ember packs (500 / 1,500 / 4,000 Ember) and NOSLOW+ (removes interstitial ads). All transactions are processed securely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your credit card or billing information.

5. Children's privacy

NOSLOW is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

6. Data retention

We do not run our own servers and do not store your gameplay data ourselves. Best scores, unlocked cosmetics, mission progress, and Ember balance are stored locally on your device and are removed when you delete the app. Data collected by AdMob, Game Center, and Apple is subject to their own retention policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete data held by third-party services. Please refer to their privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy (AdMob)
  • Apple Privacy Policy (Game Center, In-App Purchases)

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
